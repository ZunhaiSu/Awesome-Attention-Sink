### [A Unifying View of Attention Sinks: From Mechanisms to Architectural Interventions](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.08105v2)

**Source:** 2606.08105v2; checked §§3–6; Fig. 6; Table 1. **Relation:** `direct_attention_sink`.

**Finding.** Value interventions distinguish near-null NOP sinks from nonzero broadcast sinks in pretrained ViTs. Gating and a penalized position-free global pathway reorganize these computations.

**Mechanistic Interpretation:** Softmax Limitations and No-Op Theory; Other Mechanistic Interpretations.

**Strategic Mitigation:** Gated Attention Mechanisms; Pre-training Interventions; Other Mitigation Techniques.

**Classification.** NOP supports the softmax/no-op category; broadcast is retained as a fine-grained mechanistic tag. Output gating, the common-mode training penalty, and the global pathway support the three mitigation labels respectively.

**Fine-grained terms:** Adaptive NOP; Broadcast; Penalized global pathway.

**Limits.** Registers relocate both regimes. Gating increases broadcast-like sinks in the tested ViT; fewer sinks alone do not imply better representations. The v2 title replaces Two Algorithms, Two Solutions.

### [Attention Sinks in VLMs Are Borrowed, Not Seen](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.15855v2)

**Source:** 2610.15855v2; checked §§3.2–3.4, 4–5; Table 2. **Relation:** `direct_attention_sink`.

**Finding.** Centered alignment with the text-sink key identifies visual sinks. A key-direction edit cleans the map used for an entropy-triggered crop-and-relook procedure.

**Fundamental Utilization:** Attention Redistribution.

**Mechanistic Interpretation:** Implicit Attention Bias.

**Classification.** The edit calibrates an existing model's attention; it is not training prevention. The shared text-sink key direction supports Implicit Attention Bias.

**Fine-grained terms:** Text-sink key alignment; Training-free map cleaning.

**Limits.** Removed visual attention mostly returns to the text sink rather than the target. Gemma-4 reads its visual sinks; small sink-free models can be harmed. The answering pass is unedited.

### [Before Attention Sinks Form: Hidden-State Geometry Reveals and Modulates Their Formation](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.16745v1)

**Source:** 2610.16745v1; checked §§3.3, 5–8; Tables 3–4. **Relation:** `direct_attention_sink`.

**Finding.** Norm-matched singular-value interventions after a layer alter later position-0 sink behavior while preserving the current layer's attention.

**Mechanistic Interpretation:** Other Mechanistic Interpretations.

**Classification.** Pre-sink spectral geometry is a mechanistic subtopic, not automatically Geometric Anchoring. Diagnostic causal edits are not presented as a validated mitigation policy.

**Fine-grained terms:** Pre-sink spectral geometry.

**Limits.** Effects and their signs depend on model and cohort. Some models lack an identifiable pre-sink interval; longer contexts can remove onset variation.

### [It's Not RoPE that Creates Sinks: The Role of Self-Concentration and Value-Non-Mixing in Attention](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.09085v1)

**Source:** 2609.09085v1; checked §4.1–4.3; §5 and Limitations. **Relation:** `direct_attention_sink`.

**Finding.** Forcing self-concentration at a later position creates sinks and massive activations. Repeated-token experiments probe value non-mixing without forced self-attention.

**Mechanistic Interpretation:** Other Mechanistic Interpretations.

**Classification.** Value non-mixing is preserved as a specific mechanistic explanation; the study does not introduce a trained sink-removal method.

**Fine-grained terms:** Causal-mask self-concentration; Value non-mixing.

**Limits.** Key-side RoPE perturbations have limited effects under the tested intervention; this does not exclude all positional mechanisms. Repeated-token support is weaker and input-dependent in two of five models.

### [TSGate: Timestep-Aware Gated Attention for Diffusion Transformers](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.34539v1)

**Source:** 2609.34539v1; checked §§3.1–3.2, 4.1–4.2, 5; Appendix A.4. **Relation:** `direct_attention_sink`.

**Finding.** TSGate adds a timestep-conditioned offset to content gate logits before scaling value-aggregated attention features, addressing template sinks and weak early text interaction.

**Strategic Mitigation:** Gated Attention Mechanisms.

**Classification.** This is trained output-feature gating. Its time bias is not a bias on attention logits, and the gate does not renormalize the current attention matrix.

**Fine-grained terms:** Timestep-aware output gating.

**Limits.** Sink suppression alone does not restore early text guidance or generation quality. Reported gains concern the evaluated prompt formats and DiT recipe.

### [ValueDiff: Value-Geometric KV Cache Eviction for Sink-Suppressed LLMs](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.23314v2)

**Source:** 2609.23314v2; checked §§3.1–3.2, 4.4; Fig. 2. **Relation:** `sink_dependent_application`.

**Finding.** ValueDiff retains geometrically distinctive cached values using distance from their mean, motivated by changing key/value geometry in sink-suppressed models.

**Classification.** Included as sink-dependent inference research. None of the four utilization subtypes precisely describes value-based eviction without sink preservation, so those labels remain unassigned.

**Fine-grained terms:** Value-geometric KV eviction; Weak-sink deployment.

**Limits.** The method does not train away sinks. Benefits relative to key-based eviction depend on BOS geometry and relative value dispersion; the source reports exceptions.

### [Underscoring the Problem: Why Softpick Fails at Initialization](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.05488v1)

**Source:** 2610.05488v1; checked §§3–5; Table 1. **Relation:** `sink_mitigation_followup`.

**Finding.** Separating Softpick's forward and backward denominators exposes zero-support rows and tests a stop-gradient variant retaining the full forward denominator.

**Mechanistic Interpretation:** Other Mechanistic Interpretations.

**Strategic Mitigation:** Modified Softmax Functions.

**Classification.** Modified normalization and its training gradient belong to Modified Softmax Functions. Gradient-path failure is retained as a distinct mechanistic subtopic.

**Fine-grained terms:** Softpick denominator decomposition; Zero-support rows.

**Limits.** The comparison is small-scale with one checkpoint per cell. Retrieval gains at 230M do not establish general long-context robustness; both stable operators fail beyond training context.

### [Text Template Tokens Are Implicit Semantic Registers in Diffusion Transformers](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.19139v2)

**Source:** 2607.19139v2; checked §§4.1–4.5; Table 2; §6. **Relation:** `direct_attention_sink`.

**Finding.** Template sinks acquire semantics through the image stream inside the DiT despite weakly semantic input embeddings. Causal head analysis motivates training-free head pruning.

**Fundamental Utilization:** Sink Token Repurposing.

**Mechanistic Interpretation:** Other Mechanistic Interpretations.

**Classification.** The efficiency method reuses sink/register-associated functional structure rather than eliminating it. Implicit semantic registers are retained as a fine-grained mechanistic label.

**Fine-grained terms:** Implicit semantic registers; Causal head pruning.

**Limits.** Register function depends on head, depth, and trajectory. A template-sink ranking prunes more load-bearing heads than the proposed semantic-attention ranking; perceptual quality drops faster than object accuracy.

### [Massive Spikes in LLMs are Bias Vectors: Mechanistic Uncovering and Spike-Free Quantization](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.02288v1)

**Source:** 2606.02288v1; checked §§3.1–3.4, 4.1–4.3, 5. **Relation:** `direct_attention_sink`.

**Finding.** Post-normalization spike states act as bias prototypes with sink keys and near-null values. InsertQuant clamps spikes but reinstates calibrated K/V templates and terminal residual effects.

**Fundamental Utilization:** Sink Token Preservation.

**Mechanistic Interpretation:** Softmax Limitations and No-Op Theory; Outlier Circuits; Implicit Attention Bias.

**Classification.** Bias-vector geometry and value drains support the interpretation labels. Restoring the sink's functional K/V effect is preservation, even though activation spikes are removed during PTQ.

**Fine-grained terms:** Bias Vector Hypothesis; InsertQuant; K/V template restoration.

**Limits.** Spike-free quantization is not equivalent to training a sink-free model. Prototype rigidity and RoPE stability are properties of the tested models, not universal semantic irrelevance of sinks.

### [When Do Attention Circuits Form? Developmental Trajectories of Capability and Attention-Sink Emergence Across Three 1B-ClassArchitectures](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.02378v2)

**Source:** 2606.02378v2; checked §§5.1, 6, 11.2. **Relation:** `direct_attention_sink`.

**Finding.** A developmental checkpoint panel finds model-dependent BOS-attractor trajectories and mid-layer-outward emergence in Pythia, OLMo, and OLMoE.

**Mechanistic Interpretation:** Other Mechanistic Interpretations.

**Classification.** Training-dynamics observations inform mechanistic interpretation; checkpoint tracking is not itself a pre-training mitigation intervention.

**Fine-grained terms:** Developmental sink trajectories.

**Limits.** Claims depend on the selectivity threshold and checkpoint grid. Data, architecture, and training budgets co-vary; the panel does not isolate a general causal effect of corpus or MoE design.

### [The Life Cycle of a Massive Activation: Stochastic Birth, Weight-Decay-Driven Growth, and Competitive Consolidation](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.00423v2)

**Source:** 2610.00423v2; checked §§4.2–4.7, 5. **Relation:** `direct_attention_sink`.

**Finding.** Continuation and perturbation studies separate decay-driven activation-scale turnover, stochastic channel identity, collective RMS effects, and AdamW-supported maintenance of a sink-associated cohort.

**Mechanistic Interpretation:** Outlier Circuits.

**Classification.** Outlier dynamics and joint channel ablations inform Outlier Circuits. Optimizer analyses are not a demonstrated sink-free optimizer proposal.

**Fine-grained terms:** Stochastic massive-activation lifecycle; Collective RMS; Optimizer maintenance.

**Limits.** Magnitude-matched controls are strongest in small, weakly isolated models. The mechanism selecting eventual surviving channels remains unresolved.

### [Abstention and Noise Filtering: Two Missing Primitives of Softmax Attention](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.22005v2)

**Source:** 2609.22005v2; checked §§3.1–3.3, 4.3–4.4, 6. **Relation:** `direct_attention_sink`.

**Finding.** Controlled attention variants separate routing abstention from content-dependent value filtering, comparing gates, off-by-one normalization, and learned sink logits.

**Mechanistic Interpretation:** Softmax Limitations and No-Op Theory.

**Strategic Mitigation:** Gated Attention Mechanisms; Modified Softmax Functions; Learnable Attention Bias.

**Classification.** A fixed null denominator, a learnable per-head null logit, and value gates support distinct mitigation labels. Their benefits overlap partly rather than being identical.

**Fine-grained terms:** Abstention versus noise filtering; Phantom null position.

**Limits.** Value-only gates can retain position-0 attention sinks. Results vary with scale, so gate placement and sink counts cannot be collapsed into one explanation.

### [Information-Regularized Attention for Visual-Centric Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.00434v1)

**Source:** 2607.00434v1; checked §§3.2–3.4, 4.7; Tables 1, 6. **Relation:** `direct_attention_sink`.

**Finding.** IRA uses a learned stochastic value-state representation and uncertainty-weighted KL regularization during full-parameter SFT; the reported sink ratio decreases relative to ordinary SFT.

**Strategic Mitigation:** Other Mitigation Techniques.

**Classification.** Fine-tuning auxiliary regularization and value modification fit Other Mitigation Techniques. The evaluated method is neither training-free nor a pre-training intervention.

**Fine-grained terms:** Tuning auxiliary loss; Stochastic visual value regularization.

**Limits.** The paper evaluates models up to 8B and uses a particular layer-averaged visual attention threshold. Larger-scale and pre-training applications are proposed rather than established.

### [Hybrid Gated Attention](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.11805v2)

**Source:** 2608.11805v2; checked §§3.1, 3.3–3.4, 4.3–4.5. **Relation:** `direct_attention_sink`.

**Finding.** HyGA fuses input-based and post-attention gates with cross-head modulation, and combines them with a learnable null sink for stability.

**Strategic Mitigation:** Gated Attention Mechanisms; Learnable Attention Bias.

**Classification.** Content/output gates and a GPT-OSS-style learnable sink are separate architectural components; the latter is a null bias rather than a learnable prefix token.

**Fine-grained terms:** Hybrid gate fusion; Cross-head gating; Learnable null sink.

**Limits.** The paper does not establish that gating alone eliminates all sinks. Gate fusion avoids excessive attenuation, and capability gains reflect the combined design.

### [SinkPruner: Sink-Free Visual Token Pruning for Multimodal Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.01004v1)

**Source:** 2609.01004v1; checked §§3.2–3.3; Appendix H.1–H.2. **Relation:** `direct_attention_sink`.

**Finding.** SinkPruner pools high-norm visual outliers into a proxy, selects diverse remaining tokens, then applies text-guided pruning in the frozen MLLM.

**Fundamental Utilization:** Attention Redistribution.

**Classification.** This is sink-aware token-selection calibration within Attention Redistribution, not explicit mass-preserving transfer or training-based mitigation.

**Fine-grained terms:** Training-free visual sanitization; Sink-aware token pruning.

**Limits.** High norm is an operational detector, not a universal sink definition. The method retains a pooled outlier proxy; it does not simply remove every sink-associated state.

### [Decoding the Functional Roles of Register and High-Norm Patch Tokens in Vision Transformers](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.03698v1)

**Source:** 2610.03698v1; checked §§3, 4.3, 5.1. **Relation:** `related_register_outlier`.

**Finding.** Token-specific SAEs and latent interventions compare register features with high-norm patch features in DINOv2, exposing different representation-stability effects.

**Mechanistic Interpretation:** Other Mechanistic Interpretations.

**Classification.** Included as related register/outlier functional evidence. The source does not identify a complete sink-routing circuit, so the label remains Other Mechanistic Interpretations.

**Fine-grained terms:** Register/outlier SAE comparison.

**Limits.** Reported semantic and causal analyses focus on DINOv2 Small layer 8. High norm, SAE semantics, and attention sinks must not be treated as equivalent; qualitative visualizations are exploratory.

### [Which the Eye Fears: Writing with Read-Blindness Explains Massive Activations in Transformers](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.35630v1)

**Source:** 2609.35630v1; checked §§3, 4.1–4.2, 6–7. **Relation:** `related_register_outlier`.

**Finding.** Massive coordinates show asymmetric read/write access. Orthogonal or frozen read projections cause blindness to move to other pathways rather than remove massive activations.

**Mechanistic Interpretation:** Outlier Circuits.

**Classification.** Projection, FFN, and optimizer analyses concern outlier mechanisms. Partial suppression under a diagnostic intervention is not coded as a successful sink-removal technique.

**Fine-grained terms:** Read-blindness; Read/write asymmetry.

**Limits.** Massive-activation maintenance is not proof of a universal function of attention sinks. Local loss geometry and next-step optimizer evidence do not by themselves establish global training dynamics.

### [Massive Activations Are Architecturally Robust: A Controlled Scratch/Commitment Residual Stream Test](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.20743v1)

**Source:** 2606.20743v1; checked §§3.1, 5.1–5.4, 6–7. **Relation:** `related_register_outlier`.

**Finding.** Ledger Residuals introduce a protected decode-only stream, but start-token fixed-dimension outliers reappear there; stricter commit sparsity increases their concentration.

**Mechanistic Interpretation:** Outlier Circuits.

**Classification.** This is a negative architectural test of an outlier hypothesis, not evidence that the proposed protected stream successfully mitigates sinks.

**Fine-grained terms:** Ledger Residuals; Negative architectural intervention.

**Limits.** One seed per configuration at 160M/290M; outlier statistics are not equivalent to direct attention-sink measurement. The study does not distinguish sink, bias, and gradient-regulation functions.

### [Attention Sinks and Outliers in Attention Residuals](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.17887v2)

**Source:** 2605.17887v2; checked §§4.2–4.3, 6.2–6.4, 7. **Relation:** `direct_attention_sink`.

**Finding.** OASIS adds Softmax1 null routes at token and depth levels, then adjusts depth logits using centered token-level null probabilities.

**Mechanistic Interpretation:** Softmax Limitations and No-Op Theory.

**Strategic Mitigation:** Modified Softmax Functions; Other Mitigation Techniques.

**Classification.** Softmax1 supplies explicit null capacity; token-to-depth coupling is retained as a separate mitigation detail. It changes trained routing rather than reusing existing sink positions.

**Fine-grained terms:** Dual normalization; Token-to-depth null coupling.

**Limits.** Earlier omission: first submitted in May, revised in September. Null routing attenuates real branch contributions; a depth null route is not automatically an identity residual. Theory is conditional and sink visualizations are partly qualitative.

### [Sink-Sparse Hybrid Attention for Efficient Inference in T2I/T2V Diffusion Transformers](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.22975v1)

**Source:** 2610.22975v1; checked §§3.2, 4.2–4.3, 5.5–6. **Relation:** `sink_dependent_application`.

**Finding.** Offline calibration builds reusable high-mass block masks; frozen DiTs use a sparse sink branch plus a pooled-KV compensation branch.

**Fundamental Utilization:** Sink Token Preservation.

**Classification.** The method preserves existing high-mass support for inference acceleration. It neither trains a sink-free model nor adds learned prefix tokens.

**Fine-grained terms:** Static sink-support masks; Pooled-KV compensation.

**Limits.** The source uses sink broadly for recurring high-mass support, not necessarily semantically irrelevant tokens. Masks omit some useful context; compensation matters most at aggressive sparsity.

### [Spectral Geometry of Attention: From Information Routing to Uncertainty](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.06012v1)

**Source:** 2610.06012v1; checked §§3.1.1–3.1.2, 4, 5.1, 6. **Relation:** `direct_attention_sink`.

**Finding.** A probability-weighted Token Difference Operator separates sink-biased column mass from centered routing structure and supports an uncertainty estimator.

**Mechanistic Interpretation:** Other Mechanistic Interpretations.

**Classification.** Operator geometry is retained as its own mechanistic subtopic. Geometric language alone does not justify the narrower Geometric Anchoring category.

**Fine-grained terms:** Token Difference Operator; Intrinsic probability geometry.

**Limits.** High sink mass need not imply routing collapse; output dimensionality also depends on values. Uncertainty estimation does not demonstrate sink removal or improved generation accuracy.

### [Global Ranks Survive, Selected Heads Shift: BOS-Sink Topology under 4-bit Weight-Only Quantization](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.23585v1)

**Source:** 2609.23585v1; checked §§IV-A–IV-E, VI–VII. **Relation:** `direct_attention_sink`.

**Finding.** NF4 quantization preserves global BOS-sink head rankings better than discrete top-k sets; calibration-domain shifts and layer-local drift can be substantial.

**Mechanistic Interpretation:** Other Mechanistic Interpretations.

**Classification.** Included as sink-topology deployment evidence. Measuring or recalibrating sink maps is not sink mitigation, and no subtype is forced onto the study.

**Fine-grained terms:** Sink Topology Consistency; Quantized calibration transfer.

**Limits.** Three small models, one quantizer and domain pair; topology churn is not shown to impair downstream task quality. Calibration sample sufficiency is an empirical plateau criterion, not a universal threshold.

### [Layers, Sinks, and Scaling: Adaptive Evidence Selection for Multimodal Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.16795v1)

**Source:** 2609.16795v1; checked §§3.2–3.6, 4–5. **Relation:** `sink_dependent_application`.

**Finding.** AREA filters sink-contaminated visual relevance, scales crops/evidence using entropy, and uses sink mass to gate intervention in frozen MLLMs.

**Fundamental Utilization:** Attention Redistribution.

**Classification.** Sink-aware relevance calibration belongs to Attention Redistribution. It changes evidence presentation rather than transferring attention mass directly or updating weights.

**Fine-grained terms:** Sink-filtered evidence readout; Adaptive evidence allocation.

**Limits.** Activation-based sink detection and layer groups are backbone-specific. Visual intervention can substitute a crop for the original image; the full policy includes textual refresh and stream-history thresholds.

### [LASER: A Corrective Lens for LVLMs via Visual Attention Preservation and Sink Suppression](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.01707v1)

**Source:** 2607.01707v1; checked §§4.2–4.5, 5.3–5.4, 6. **Relation:** `direct_attention_sink`.

**Finding.** LASER trains with GRPO rewards combining sustained non-sink visual attention and suppression of disproportionate visual sink attention, gated by answer correctness.

**Strategic Mitigation:** Other Mitigation Techniques.

**Classification.** Sink-dependent reinforcement-learning rewards are a tuning intervention under Other Mitigation Techniques. The method is not a training-free attention edit.

**Fine-grained terms:** Tuning auxiliary reward; Visual grounding and sink suppression.

**Limits.** High-activation dimensions define candidate sinks in the evaluated backbones. Grounding rewards alone can increase sink attention, so the combined result cannot be attributed to visual persistence alone.
